About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a leading non-profit organization dedicated to creating positive social change through community-driven initiatives. Established on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, the foundation was founded with a vision to build a society where every individual has access to opportunities, resources, and support needed to live a dignified life. As a Section 8 registered organization, InAmigos Foundation operates with a commitment to transparency, accountability, and sustainable development. The organization has grown into a nationwide movement supported by volunteers, interns, social workers, educators, and community leaders who share a common goal of improving lives and empowering communities. The foundation believes that meaningful change begins at the grassroots level. Through a variety of social welfare programs, awareness campaigns, educational initiatives, environmental projects, and skill development opportunities, InAmigos Foundation works tirelessly to address some of the most pressing challenges faced by society. Its efforts are guided by the principles of compassion, inclusivity, equality, and service to humanity. The organization strives to create long-term impact rather than short-term solutions, ensuring that beneficiaries gain the knowledge, skills, and confidence necessary to improve their own lives and contribute positively to society.

Vision

The vision of InAmigos Foundation is to create an inclusive, compassionate, and empowered society where every individual has equal opportunities to succeed regardless of their social, economic, or geographical background. The foundation envisions a future where education is accessible to all, women are empowered to achieve financial independence, animals are treated with kindness and respect, and communities actively participate in environmental conservation and social development.

Mission

The mission of InAmigos Foundation is to promote sustainable development through initiatives focused on education, women empowerment, environmental protection, animal welfare, community support, and skill development. The organization aims to bridge social and economic gaps by providing resources, opportunities, and support systems that enable individuals and communities to thrive. Through partnerships, volunteer engagement, and innovative programs, the foundation seeks to create measurable and lasting social impact across India.
